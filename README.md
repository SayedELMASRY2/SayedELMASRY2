I am a **Big Data & Data Engineer** with a strong mathematical foundation from **Mansoura University (B.Sc. in Mathematics)**. I specialize in designing and engineering high-throughput, fault-tolerant **Medallion Lakehouse architectures**, **real-time streaming systems**, and **scalable analytical data platforms**.
